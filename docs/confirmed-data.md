|---|---|---|

| Size of the meta-analysis | 462 experiments, 11,768 observations | Same |

## ✏️ Corrections to the original documents
- Coffee is **not 74 ha**: it is ~**74,000 ha**.
- Rice after legumes: **15.7 %** and **65.8 %**, not 15 % and 65.5 %.
- "The GIBS API is paused" is false: what closed was **NEO**.
- El Niño is no longer a projection: it is happening and it is **stronger** (−37 %) than the document said (~−30 %).
- The "+300 % prices in 2014-15" has no source: use the verified 2026 figures (tomato +174 %, onion +60.6 %).
