How do I know my texture?

Ribbon test: wet a handful of soil and squeeze it between your thumb and index finger. If it does not form a ribbon, it is sandy. If it forms a short ribbon that breaks (less than 2.5 cm), it is loam. If it forms a long, shiny ribbon, it is clay.
